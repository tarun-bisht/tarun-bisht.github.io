SELECTED RESEARCH PROJECTS

NLP for Optimization Problems, Techfest 2023

Modelling optimization problems from natural language text descriptions into solver-compatible modelling language. A key emphasis is leveraging language models and extracting valuable representations from scarce data resources due to the labour-intensive nature of annotating data for optimization problems.

Direct speech-to-speech translation with Voice Interpolation, WiSSAP 2023

Direct speech-to-speech translation between languages, bypassing text as an intermediate step. Designed a latent space to represent and interpolate speaker voice characteristics, enabling dynamic voice modifications. Generated speech variations for both the same speaker and different speakers. Focused on enhancing naturalness and flexibility in multilingual voice synthesis.

NeurIPS Large Language Model Efficiency Challenge, NeurIPS Challenge 2023

Adapted a foundation model to specialized tasks by fine-tuning on a single GPU (4090 or A100 40GB) within a 24-hour timeframe. Secured a top 10 ranking in the 4090 GPU track.

AI-based surveillance system, Kumaun University 2020

Developed an affordable alert system for detecting monkey theft with high accuracy by training advanced single-shot object detection algorithms. Optimized the system’s performance by converting the trained model to TensorFlow Lite, increasing detection speed from 1 FPS to 24 FPS, enabling deployment on Raspberry Pi. Additionally, provided a software solution compatible with existing IP cameras.
